# Changelog

## 2026-09-22

### WolfSniperHit 0.1.1

- Trigger only after a real local-player ranged hit.
- Reject NPC-originated hits, including albatross attacks.
- Support normal-hit mode and optional kill-only mode.

### AlbatrossWolfHit 0.1.0

- Initial release.
- Trigger only when an albatross NPC projectile hits the local player.
- Reject player weapon hits and Bowhead Whale lava projectiles.
